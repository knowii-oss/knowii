# Data processing agreement

This data processing agreement ("DPA") applies between the owner of a community on Knowii (the "Controller") and DeveloPassion SRL, 126 Route de Mons, 7130 Binche, Belgium, company number 0717.561.755 (the "Processor"). It is part of the [terms of service](/terms-of-service) and follows article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The Controller accepts it when it creates a community.

For the Knowii accounts themselves, DeveloPassion SRL is the controller: see the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).

## 1. Subject and duration

The Processor hosts the community of the Controller on Knowii and processes the personal data of its members for this purpose only. The DPA applies as long as the community exists on Knowii.

## 2. Nature and purpose of the processing

Storage, display, transmission and deletion of the data of the community, to run the community features: spaces, posts, comments, reactions, resources, chat, direct messages, courses, invitations, access groups, real-time updates and the emails of the service.

## 3. Categories of data and of data subjects

1. Data subjects: the members of the community, the people it invites, and the visitors of its public spaces.
2. Data: name, username, email address, avatar, the profile fields that each member fills in, roles and access groups, the content that members post and the images they upload, messages, reactions, and technical data (IP address, browser) for security.

The Controller does not ask its members for special categories of data (GDPR art. 9) through Knowii.

## 4. Obligations of the Processor

1. The Processor processes the data only on the documented instructions of the Controller. The terms of service, this DPA and the settings of the community are these instructions. If an instruction breaks the GDPR, the Processor tells the Controller.
2. The people who have access to the data are bound by confidentiality.
3. The Processor applies the security measures of section 6.
4. The Processor helps the Controller to answer the requests of data subjects (access, correction, deletion, portability), to secure the data, to notify breaches and to carry out impact assessments, in proportion to the information it has.
5. The Processor gives the Controller the information needed to show that this DPA is respected. The Controller can audit the Processor once a year, at its own cost, with 30 days of notice, or have an independent auditor do it under confidentiality.

## 6. Security measures

1. Encryption in transit (HTTPS for every connection) and of the backups.
2. Passwords stored as hashes, two-factor authentication available, sign-in links valid for {{retention.sign_in_link}} and used once.
3. Access control in the application: every request is checked against the community, the role and the access groups of the member.
4. A strict Content Security Policy, rate limits, and bot checks on the public forms.
5. Access to the servers limited to the people who run Knowii.
6. Server logs kept for {{retention.logs}}. Daily backups, kept as {{retention.backups_daily}} daily, {{retention.backups_weekly}} weekly and {{retention.backups_monthly}} monthly snapshots.

## 7. Personal data breaches

The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay, and at most 48 hours after it becomes aware of a personal data breach that affects the data of the community. The notification describes the breach, the data and the people concerned, the likely consequences and the measures taken.

## 8. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some sub-processors are in the United States. These transfers use the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. The United Kingdom has an adequacy decision.

## 9. End of the processing

When the Controller deletes its community, the Processor deletes its data at once, including the uploaded files. Copies in backups are deleted when the backup is deleted, at most {{retention.backups_longest}} later. The Controller can export its data before the deletion, through the API.

## 10. Applicable law

Belgian law applies to this DPA. Any legal action about this DPA goes to the Belgian courts, which have exclusive jurisdiction: the courts of the registered office of DeveloPassion SRL.
