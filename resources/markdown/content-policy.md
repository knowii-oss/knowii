# Content policy

This policy tells what you may post on Knowii, what happens with your content, and how to report content that breaks the rules or the law. It is part of the [terms of service](/terms-of-service).

## 1. Your content stays yours

You keep the rights to what you post. You give DeveloPassion SRL the right to store, show and send your content to the members who have access to it, only to run the service. This right ends when the content is deleted, except for the copies in backups, which are deleted in turn (see the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy)).

You can edit and delete your posts, comments and messages. When you delete your account, your personal data goes, and your content stays under the name "Deleted member".

## 2. What you may not post

1. Illegal content, and content that encourages illegal acts.
2. Harassment, threats, and content that attacks people for their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
3. Sexual content that involves minors, of any kind. We report it to the authorities.
4. Personal data of other people without their consent (addresses, phone numbers, private messages, photos).
5. Impersonation: do not pretend to be another person or organisation.
6. Spam: unsolicited advertising, repeated messages, link schemes, and automated posting without the agreement of the community.
7. Malware, phishing, and links to them.
8. Content that you have no right to share, for example the full text of a book or a paid course.

Each community can add its own rules. Its moderators apply them.

## 3. How moderation works

The moderators of a community act first: they can delete content and remove members in their community. The owner of the community decides who moderates. DeveloPassion SRL acts on reports of illegal content and on content that breaks this policy, in every community.

When we remove your content or restrict your account, we tell you why, which rule applies, and how you can contest the decision.

## 4. How to report content

For content that breaks the rules of a community, contact its moderators.

For illegal content, or content that breaks this policy, write to [contact@developassion.be](mailto:contact@developassion.be) with:

1. The exact address (URL) of the content.
2. Why you think that the content is illegal or breaks this policy.
3. Your name and email address (not required for content that involves the sexual abuse of children).
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the information in your report is correct and complete.

We confirm that we received the report, examine it, and tell you our decision. This email address is also the point of contact for the authorities of the EU member states, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services, under articles 11 and 12 of the Digital Services Act. You can write to us in English or French.

## 5. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top.
