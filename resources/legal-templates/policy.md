## 2. The data we collect

1. Account: your name, email address, username, password (stored as a hash, never in clear text), two-factor authentication secret and recovery codes (encrypted), avatar, the date you verified your email address, and a new email address that you asked for, with the date you asked, until you confirm it or cancel it, or for {{retention.pending_email}} at most, and your choice to show or hide your online status, and, when you sign in with the sign-in provider of this site ({{sign_in_provider}}), the identifier of that account and the date you connected it, and the time of a new sign-in with it when you confirm the deletion of an account that has no password; your time zone, which your browser gives when you sign up or which you set yourself, to show times in your zone; the date of your last visit (a date without an hour, replaced at each visit, that nobody else can see), to show you what is new since then; and your choice to show or hide pop-ups.
2. Profile: what you choose to add (headline, bio, location, phone number, links to your sites and social accounts), and which of these fields other members see. Your phone number is hidden by default.
3. Communities: your memberships, your role, your access groups, the tags that the managers of a community put on you, the lessons of a course that you marked as done, your answers and scores in the quizzes of lessons, the members you blocked, the invitations sent to your email address, with the access groups and the tags that they give when you join, the invite link through which you joined a community, if you used one, and the date, what you read in chat spaces and conversations, to show what is new, the threads that you follow or muted and the level that you chose for each space (Everything, Only what is for me, or Muted), which Knowii keeps while you are a member of the community and which you can change at any time, and the notifications that tell you when a member replies to you or mentions you, or when something new happens in a thread that you follow, in a space where you chose Everything, or in a course that you started (who wrote, about which post, comment, message or lesson, and when you saw it), and the date and hour of your last visit of each space, to mark the spaces that have something new for you, and, when you sign in with the sign-in provider of this site, the list of what your account there gives access to, which this site reads when you sign in, when it changes, and once a night, to open or close the spaces of a community. If a manager of a community removes you from it, Knowii keeps a record of that community, your account and the date, so that you cannot join it again by yourself; the record is deleted when you accept a new invitation, when a manager lets you join again, when the community is deleted or when you delete your account.
4. Your content: posts, comments, reactions, mentions, resources and their captured content, chat messages, direct messages, pages, lessons, events (their title, description, date, place and meeting address), and uploaded images and their alternative text. Uploaded images are re-encoded on our server, which removes their metadata (location, camera).
5. Technical data: the IP address and the browser of each session, the API tokens you create and the date of their last use, the token of the 'Remember me' option, your password reset requests until they expire, and counters by IP address that limit the number of requests (rate limits) and, for each invitation email that you send, the community and the time, kept {{retention.invitation_mails}}, to limit the invitation emails of an account and of a community.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

## 4. Who receives the data

Members of a community see what you post in it, and the profile fields you choose to show. The members of a community see its member list: your name, your username, your picture, your headline, your role and the date you joined. The members of a community see the tags that its managers chose to show, on your profile and in the member list there. The owner, the administrators and the moderators who manage members see all your tags in that community. Your data export lists all of them. The owner and the administrators of a community see who joined it through each of its invite links.

What you post in a public space of a community can be read by anyone on the internet, with your name and your picture.

## 5. How long we keep the data

1. Account and profile: while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data at once. Your posts, comments, resources and messages stay where you wrote them, under the name "Deleted member". When you delete your account, you can also delete your posts, comments and messages. Afterwards, write to {{contact}} to have content removed.
2. Content: until its author, a moderator or the owner deletes it, or the community is deleted. A deleted message, and a deleted post or comment that other people answered, leave an empty marker without their text.
3. Sessions: {{retention.session}} after your last activity. Sign-in links: {{retention.sign_in_link}}. Password reset links: {{retention.password_reset_link}}.
4. Invitations: valid for {{retention.invitation}}, deleted {{retention.expired_invitations}} after they expire. The record of an import from a file: deleted {{retention.invitation_imports}} after the invitations were sent, or {{retention.invitation_import_previews}} after the upload when nobody sent them.
5. API tokens: until they expire ({{retention.api_token_default}} by default) or you revoke them. Expired tokens are deleted {{retention.expired_api_tokens}} after their expiry date.
6. Uploaded images that no content uses: {{retention.unused_uploads}}.
7. Server logs: {{retention.logs}}. Failed background tasks: {{retention.failed_jobs}}.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion, limit or object to its use, and receive it in a machine-readable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time. You correct your profile and delete your account yourself in the settings. For the other requests, write to [{{contact}}](mailto:{{contact}}). We answer within one month.

We make no automated decisions about you, and we do no profiling.

For data in a community, you can also contact its owner.

## 9. People who are not members

Content in a community, such as a post or a captured article, can name people who are not members of Knowii. The owner of the community decides about that content. If it is about you, write to {{contact}}: we pass your request to the owner and remove the content when the law requires it.

## 10. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we inform the members by email before the change applies.
