# Privacy policy

This policy explains which personal data Knowii collects, why, how long it keeps it, and what your rights are. It applies to the Knowii platform and to the communities that it hosts.

## 1. Who is responsible

DeveloPassion SRL, 126 Route de Mons, 7130 Binche, Belgium (company number 0717.561.755, VAT BE0717561755) runs Knowii. The contact person for data protection is Sébastien Dubois, founder of DeveloPassion SRL. Contact: [contact@developassion.be](mailto:contact@developassion.be).

Knowii is for people of 16 and older. We do not knowingly keep the data of younger people: write to us and we delete the account.

DeveloPassion SRL is the controller of the data of your account on this site. For the content and the members of a community, the owner of the community decides what happens, and DeveloPassion SRL processes the data on the owner's behalf, as described in the [data processing agreement](/dpa).

## 2. The data we collect

1. Account: your name, email address, username, password (stored as a hash, never in clear text), two-factor authentication secret and recovery codes (encrypted), avatar, the date you verified your email address, and a new email address that you asked for, with the date you asked, until you confirm it or cancel it, or for {{retention.pending_email}} at most, and your choice to show or hide your online status, and, when you sign in with the sign-in provider of this site ({{sign_in_provider}}), the identifier of that account and the date you connected it, and the time of a new sign-in with it when you confirm the deletion of an account that has no password; your time zone, which your browser gives when you sign up or which you set yourself, to show times in your zone; the date of your last visit (a date without an hour, replaced at each visit, that nobody else can see), to show you what is new since then; and your choice to show or hide pop-ups.
2. Profile: what you choose to add (headline, bio, location, phone number, links to your sites and social accounts), and which of these fields other members see. Your phone number is hidden by default.
3. Communities: your memberships, your role, your access groups, the tags that the managers of a community put on you, the lessons of a course that you marked as done, your answers and scores in the quizzes of lessons, the members you blocked, the invitations sent to your email address, what you read in chat spaces and conversations, to show what is new, the threads that you follow or muted and the level that you chose for each space (Everything, Only what is for me, or Muted), which Knowii keeps while you are a member of the community and which you can change at any time, and the notifications that tell you when a member replies to you or mentions you, or when something new happens in a thread that you follow, in a space where you chose Everything, or in a course that you started (who wrote, about which post, comment, message or lesson, and when you saw it), and the date and hour of your last visit of each space, to mark the spaces that have something new for you, and, when you sign in with the sign-in provider of this site, the list of what your account there gives access to, which this site reads when you sign in, when it changes, and once a night, to open or close the spaces of a community. If a manager of a community removes you from it, Knowii keeps a record of that community, your account and the date, so that you cannot join it again by yourself; the record is deleted when you accept a new invitation, when a manager lets you join again, when the community is deleted or when you delete your account.
4. Your content: posts, comments, reactions, mentions, resources and their captured content, chat messages, direct messages, pages, lessons, events (their title, description, date, place and meeting address), and uploaded images and their alternative text. Uploaded images are re-encoded on our server, which removes their metadata (location, camera).
5. Technical data: the IP address and the browser of each session, the API tokens you create and the date of their last use, the token of the 'Remember me' option, your password reset requests until they expire, and counters by IP address that limit the number of requests (rate limits).
6. Waitlist and newsletter: your email address, if you join the newsletter, or if you joined the waitlist before it closed.
7. Data that we get from other people: when a manager of a community invites you, they give us your email address. When a member mentions you, that mention is content of the community. The managers of a community can put tags on you, for example "Founder". A tag is a short name and holds no other text.
8. Audience statistics: page views, counted by Plausible without cookies and without a stored identifier. Our proxy at Cloudflare and Plausible see your IP address to count the view and do not keep it.
9. Your answers to events (yes, replay only or no), until {{retention.event_answers}} after the event or until you leave the community. The host and the team of an event see who answered yes or replay only. They do not see when you open the meeting. We do not record who attends a meeting.

Your name, email address, username and password are required to create an account. Without them, we cannot give you one. Everything else is optional.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

1. To run your account and the communities you join: the contract between you and us (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
2. To protect the platform (sign-in protection, rate limits, bot checks, logs, backups): our legitimate interest in a secure service (art. 6(1)(f)).
3. To send the newsletter emails, and the waitlist emails to the addresses given before it closed: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)). You can unsubscribe in every email.
4. To send the emails that the service needs (sign-in links, address verification, invitations): the contract.
5. To know how many people visit the pages: our legitimate interest in improving the service (art. 6(1)(f)).

## 4. Who receives the data

Members of a community see what you post in it, and the profile fields you choose to show. The members of a community see its member list: your name, your username, your picture, your headline, your role and the date you joined. The members of a community see the tags that its managers chose to show, on your profile and in the member list there. The owner, the administrators and the moderators who manage members see all your tags in that community. Your data export lists all of them.

What you post in a public space of a community can be read by anyone on the internet, with your name and your picture.

## 5. How long we keep the data

1. Account and profile: while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data at once. Your posts, comments, resources and messages stay where you wrote them, under the name "Deleted member". When you delete your account, you can also delete your posts, comments and messages. Afterwards, write to contact@developassion.be to have content removed.
2. Content: until its author, a moderator or the owner deletes it, or the community is deleted. A deleted message, and a deleted post or comment that other people answered, leave an empty marker without their text.
3. Sessions: {{retention.session}} after your last activity. Sign-in links: {{retention.sign_in_link}}. Password reset links: {{retention.password_reset_link}}.
4. Invitations: valid for {{retention.invitation}}, deleted {{retention.expired_invitations}} after they expire.
5. API tokens: until they expire ({{retention.api_token_default}} by default) or you revoke them. Expired tokens are deleted {{retention.expired_api_tokens}} after their expiry date.
6. Uploaded images that no content uses: {{retention.unused_uploads}}.
7. Server logs: {{retention.logs}}. Failed background tasks: {{retention.failed_jobs}}.
8. Waitlist: it takes no new address. The addresses given before are deleted after the launch invitation, at most {{retention.waitlist}} after you joined it. Unconfirmed and unsubscribed addresses of the waitlist and the newsletter: {{retention.inactive_contacts}} after their last change.
9. Backups: we keep {{retention.backups_daily}} daily, {{retention.backups_weekly}} weekly and {{retention.backups_monthly}} monthly encrypted backups. Deleted data can stay in a backup for up to {{retention.backups_longest}}, until that backup is deleted.
10. Online status: never stored. Other members see it only while you are connected, and only if you allow it in your profile settings.
11. Notifications: {{retention.notifications_read}} after you read them, {{retention.notifications_unread}} when you never read them.
12. Follows, mutes and space levels: while you are a member of the community.
13. Time zone and pop-up choice: while your account exists. Last visit date: replaced at each visit. Last visit of each space: replaced at each visit, and deleted when you leave the community.
14. Answers to events: {{retention.event_answers}} after the event, or until you leave the community.
15. Tags: until a manager removes the tag, you leave the community, or you delete your account.

## 6. Cookies

Knowii uses only the cookies and the browser storage that the service needs, so there is no consent banner.

| Name                                                                                 | Kind            | Purpose                                                                                                                                           | Duration                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `{{cookies.session}}`                                                                | Cookie          | Keeps you signed in                                                                                                                               | {{retention.session}} after your last activity                                        |
| `XSRF-TOKEN`                                                                         | Cookie          | Protects your forms against cross-site requests                                                                                                   | The same duration as the session                                                      |

The sign-up and newsletter forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots. Cloudflare processes your IP address and data about your browser for this check.

## 7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, and two-factor authentication is available. The pages use a strict Content Security Policy. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Only the people who run Knowii have access to the servers.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion, limit or object to its use, and receive it in a machine-readable format. You can withdraw your consent at any time. You correct your profile and delete your account yourself in the settings. For the other requests, write to [contact@developassion.be](mailto:contact@developassion.be). We answer within one month.

We make no automated decisions about you, and we do no profiling.

For data in a community, you can also contact its owner.

If you think that we do not respect your rights, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, [www.dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).

## 9. People who are not members

Content in a community, such as a post or a captured article, can name people who are not members of Knowii. The owner of the community decides about that content. If it is about you, write to contact@developassion.be: we pass your request to the owner and remove the content when the law requires it.

## 10. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we inform the members by email before the change applies.
